# Archived Wrocław GTFS sample

The archive in this directory is a derivative sample of public Wrocław GTFS,
published by Urząd Miejski Wrocławia — Wydział Transportu under **CC0 1.0**.
The official metadata and redistribution conditions were checked on 2026-10-10:
<https://open-data.cui.wroclaw.pl/hdb/metadane/13/>.
License: <https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/>.

This is an archived, filtered timetable, not an official product or the complete
network. Its original download URL, download time and SHA-256, the sample hash,
selection rules and row counts are recorded in `manifest.json`. All included
names, trips, times and shape coordinates come from that existing source.
No new download is performed by the demo.

The application's MIT license does not replace the data's CC0 dedication. Map
tiles are not included. OpenStreetMap attribution and provider terms still apply
when the optional internet basemap is enabled.
